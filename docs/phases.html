Lootwright phases

A WoW Forever addon: search an item, see which mobs drop it, where, at what level and drop rate, then farm it with map skulls, goals and recipes. Nine of ten phases are done. Renamed from ItemFinder, a name already taken on CurseForge.

Known limits

  • Normal-mob rates come from CMaNGOS classic-db (Classic 1.12, not measured in Forever) and only drops of 1% and up are kept; rarer drops still show "rate unknown". Checked against rates measured in Forever on bosses, CMaNGOS agrees on only 38% (31 of 80), so its numbers are shown as rough, like "~37.0%", and sorted after real rates. The build prints this check every time.
  • Farm skull grouping uses a fixed grid and doesn't change with map zoom.
  • Professions read only the recipes you know from your own profession windows; the full list comes from ForeverDB data.

Updated 19 November 2026 (0.10.0 released). Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), QuestieDB and CMaNGOS classic-db.
