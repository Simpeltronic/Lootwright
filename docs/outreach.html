Reddit post

On release day

Where: the WoW Forever subreddit, if there is one (check its rules on self-promotion first). Attach the Search tab, Farm tab and tracker screenshots.

Title: I made a free addon that shows every mob that drops an item, and marks them on your map (Lootwright)

I kept losing time hunting materials for my professions, so I made an addon for WoW Forever.

Type an item's name (or shift-click it) and Lootwright shows every mob that drops it: level, zone, rank and drop rate, your current zone first. Put it on your farm list with a goal and the mobs you need get a skull on the world map and a line on their tooltip until your bags hold enough.

What it does:
- Search any item, filter by reagents, quest items, gear or dungeon drops
- Farm a whole recipe: every material becomes a goal, crafted materials open up into what they're made from
- Farm tracker on screen, with bank counts
- Every recipe for every profession, including ones you haven't learned
- Dungeon boss rates measured in Forever (ForeverDB.net data)

Drop rates for normal mobs are honest about where they come from: most are rough Classic numbers, marked with ~. If you opt in, Lootwright counts what you loot (on your PC only) and you can share it, so we can work out real Forever rates together.

It needs QuestieDB, not Questie itself. Free, open source (GPL-3.0).

CurseForge: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lootwright
GitHub: https://github.com/Simpeltronic/Lootwright

It's an early version (0.10) and still growing, so feedback and bug reports are very welcome.
